# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

TorchGAMLSS contributors, maintainers, and participants are committed to a
welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Critique ideas, code, and statistical claims rather than people.
- Acknowledge sources, prior work, and contributions.
- Protect private information and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate.

Sensitive reports may be sent privately through the repository's GitHub
Security Advisory form. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential
to the extent practical.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1, available under
CC BY 4.0 at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
